Open the template in Inkscape, Adobe Illustrator or another vector graphics program.

Texture should be exported to PNG using the "Page" export area in Inkscape. Adobe Illustrator export has not been tested.


Artists making a new texture in raster graphics (using GIMP, Photoshop etc.) are on their own as I will not be making a raster template. Use the guide texture to understand the meaning of the textures.
If you choose to do this keep this in mind:

- All the white textured cards except the add card button should be in greyscale.

- 1 px clipping can happen so make sure the cards look similar where they intersect with other card textures

- The current colour scheme should support play by colour blind players. It is recommended to use a colour blind compatible colour scheme.

- Make sure to flip the colours on the backside texture.

- The second row of numbers is vertically flipped to make it easier to avoid noticable 1 px clipping.
